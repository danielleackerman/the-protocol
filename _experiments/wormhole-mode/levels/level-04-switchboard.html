Reference

System Definition

Every area of reality has a switch: on or off.

The position of each switch determines what is currently rendering in the field.

The switch position is the instruction. The system obeys the instruction.

Operator Responsibility

Most people are running their lives with half their switches off without ever knowing the switches existed.

The operator opens the switchboard and sets every switch.

Each switch is a state of being authorized to render.

Configuration Principle

The field is not reacting to wishes. The field is executing configuration.

Wishes are fake. Configurations are precise.

The system obeys the switch, not the mood present when the switch is set.

Standing Wave

The configuration runs underneath the day. Bad day or good day, the switchboard remains in its set position.
