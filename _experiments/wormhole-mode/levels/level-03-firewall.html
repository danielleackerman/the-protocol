System Definition

A breach occurs when unauthorized old code attempts to execute within your current system.

It often manifests as external triggers: messages, comments, conversations, familiar voices from the past, inherited beliefs, or people expecting a previous version of your character.

Breach Signal

When a breach occurs, the render stutters and the body may tighten.

You temporarily lose your status as the Operator and revert to the person you were years ago.

Use this when an outside message, panic, or old code tries to enter the field.

Firewall Function

The Protocol does not prevent breaches. It provides the firewall to seal them in under 30 seconds.

The firewall denies unauthorized code and restores the operator seat.
