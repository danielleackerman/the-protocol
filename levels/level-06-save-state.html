Save Peak State

Save your daily peaks and raise your floor.

Operation

Protocol Sequence

Twenty seconds. Four steps. The moment becomes the new floor, not the ceiling.

01 Recognize Say out loud: This is a peak. The recognition is the first half of the save.
02 Anchor Notice three sensory details: what you see, what you feel in the body, what you hear. Three points pin the moment to the system.
03 Save Save state. This is the new baseline. Restore here on drift. The moment becomes the new baseline.
04 Seal Save complete. Anchor locked. The return point is updated.

What Counts as a Peak

A peak can be small: a regulated meal where the body felt settled, a walk where the field felt magnetic, a balance on the screen, or a sentence someone said to you.

The moment can be small. If the render is live, save it.
