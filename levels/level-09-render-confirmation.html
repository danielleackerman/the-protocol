What Counts as a Confirmation

A confirmation is not the full outcome. The full outcome is the final render. The confirmation is the receipt arriving in between.

Signalsa sentence someone says, a repeated number, a door opening, a protective no, a free coffee, an upgrade, a kind stranger, or a feeling that something shifted before anything visible changed.

Failure Modes

MissingThe system keeps sending, then gets quieter because the receiver is closed.
Over-readingTurning every coincidence into hours of decoding means you left the operator seat.
