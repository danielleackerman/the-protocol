System Definition

Every area of reality has a switch: on or off. The switch position is the instruction.

Operator Responsibility

The operator opens the switchboard and sets every switch. The system obeys configuration, not mood.

Standing Wave

The configuration runs underneath the day. Bad day or good day, the switchboard remains in its set position.
