Quick Reference

All main points and commands from every day of the Wormhole — Days 01 through 14 — in one place.

WormholeDay 01

Days 01–06 / First Half

Installation

Day 01 — The Entry

Synchronized global pickup. The moment the Wormhole opens. The first hours after the live are not preparation — they are operation.

First DecisionThe first decision you make today — make it as the new character. Within two hours. Note the decision.
First MirrorStand in front of a mirror. Speak one sentence out loud about who you are now. Not an affirmation — a statement of fact. Once. No repetition.
First RedirectThe first moment today that hands you an old trigger — register it as data and operate as the new character anyway. Say ACCESS DENIED and continue.

Day 02 — Proof of Identity

The new identity ran today. It made decisions. It moved the body. The file is not an idea anymore — the file is what ran the day.

Five ProofsLog five pieces of evidence today that the new character ran — not the old one. The proof seals the switch.
DENIEDEvery time the old pattern surfaces — a reflex, a reaction, a familiar pull — run the command. DENIED. The old pattern does not land.
Three SwitchesThe File. The Body. The Field. Each locked deeper now that there is proof to seal them to.

Day 03 — Decide From the File

Every decision today comes from the new file — not from the situation, not from the trigger, not from what the old character would have done.

FROM THE FILEThe command that routes every decision through the new identity. Spoken internally before any significant response or choice.
Decision LogBy bedtime, log three decisions made from the file today. What the old character would have done. What the file decided instead.

Day 04 — Field Operations

The new character moves through the world today. The field responds to the file you are running.

PORTAL OPENBefore entering any significant space today — a room, a meeting, a conversation. The command primes the field before the body crosses the threshold.
Field LogBy bedtime, log three field responses — moments where the environment, a person, or the situation responded to the new file rather than the old one.

Day 05 — Already Done

The result is not coming. It is already done. The operator does not wait for confirmation — the operator operates from the state of receipt.

ALREADY DONEThe command that collapses the gap between source and render. Spoken when the body starts anticipating, waiting, or monitoring for arrival.
Receipt LogBy bedtime, log three moments where you operated from the state of already done — not waiting, not checking, not performing. Already there.

Day 06 — Operating From Source

Identity is source code. Reality is render. The Wormhole replaces the source. Today the body practices being the new file rather than performing it.

NOWEvery time the body slips into anticipation, projection, or replay — run the command internally. One word. One beat. Returns the body to source operation.
Drop the PerformanceThree times today, catch the performance and drop it. Let the sentence end where the new source would end it. No hedging. No qualifying. No apologizing.
I AM StatementBuild tonight. Three parts: I AM + the frequency you operate from + what reality renders from you. Speak internally. Speak out loud. Slower. Then say EXECUTE I AM. Field sealed.
Force Quit SequenceFORCE QUIT PUSHING. FORCE QUIT PERFORMANCE. FORCE QUIT CONVINCING. Programs terminated.
Execute SequenceEXECUTE: I am the source. EXECUTE: Reality renders from me. EXECUTE: I command reality to bend.

Day 07 / Midpoint

The Apex

Day 07 — The Apex

The crossing point. Six days of operation become architecture. The new file stops being something you are running and becomes the operator itself. There is no return from here.

Choose Your HourBlock time in your calendar for the Apex Ritual. Headphones. Private space. Two meters of clear floor space. Standing. Mirror preferred.
I CROSSOne full step forward. Speak at full volume as the crossing command. The step is the crossing. The architecture becomes permanent.
AT APEXSpoken out loud to lock the crossing. Panel and operator become one architecture.
NO RETURNThe slow-path timeline becomes inaccessible from the other side. On the other side, you cannot find the version of you who entered Day 01.
What locksThree switches, five commands, I AM statement, Reality Control Panel — all operational and permanent.

Days 08–13 / Second Half

Governance

Day 08 — Broadcast On

Apex has been crossed. The architecture moves into governance. The operator does not turn on the signal — the signal is on.

BROADCAST ONFirst thing upon waking. Before any device or input. Speak out loud. Then speak your I AM statement once, full weight. The day's broadcast is now active.
Space TransmissionsThree times today, at any threshold: pause one breath, broadcast your I AM statement into the space, then enter. The signal goes first. The body follows.
Five AuthorizationsNormalize. Acceleration. Expansion. Your Reality. Stability. Each spoken tonight. Each becomes binding the moment it is spoken.
Render Speed LogBy bedtime, log three moments where reality responded faster, larger, or with more synchronicity than the pre-Apex baseline.

Day 09 — Background Processing

The architecture keeps operating even when you stop staring at it. Constant conscious involvement is no longer necessary for reality to continue organizing.

CONTINUINGSpoken internally when you notice reality organizing without interference. No excitement. No chasing. Three observations minimum.
BACKGROUND PROCESSING ACTIVEWhen the body attempts to force, overcheck, anticipate, or grip — pause one breath, speak the command, release. Run as many times as needed.
Passive Render LogBy bedtime, log three moments where reality moved without force — because the architecture continued operating autonomously.

Day 10 — Portal Creation

Movement is timeline selection. Every step carries instruction. The operator walks reality into new structure.

Portal SequenceChoose a physical crossing point. Pause. Select a reality or sequence. Cross with a clap, snap, or intentional step. The movement seals the sequence. Three portals minimum.
Step. Select.Run at random walking moments. At least 10 steps. Minimum three times today. Movement carries reality instruction.
SEQUENCE CONFIRMEDSpoken aloud when crossing the final portal of the morning walk.
Sequence LogBy bedtime, log three moments where reality felt different after movement operations.

Day 11 — Response Confirmed

Reality is already responding. The render is already answering differently. Today the job is to observe — not push. Observation mission.

RESPONSE CONFIRMEDSpoken internally whenever reality responds faster, cleaner, or more directly than the pre-Apex baseline. No amplification. No proof-seeking.
Observer StateThree one-minute pauses today. No phone. No forcing. Simply observe the environment, timing, and sequence unfolding in real time.
Render Confirmation LogBy bedtime, log five render confirmations. Not manifestations. Not luck. Render confirmations.
Time LabTonight: old delay patterns and timing permission structures rewrite. The next 72 hours are already reorganizing from tonight's recalibration.

Day 12 — Proof Issuance

Reality stabilizes around what you repeatedly confirm through behavior, standards, movement, and decisions. Proof is not effort — proof is confirmation. This is where timelines lock.

PROOF ISSUEDSpoken internally after every conscious confirmation of the new sequence through behavior, decision, or movement. Three proof issuances minimum.
Switch ConfirmationWhen you notice a lower switch activating — pause, select a different switch, immediately issue one small proof to seal it.
CONTROL ROOM OPENInstant entry command to the control room interface. Spoken at any moment to access render speed, overflow calibration, and sequence dial.
Control Room LogBy bedtime, log five moments where you consciously issued proof for the updated sequence.

Day 13 — Coherence Lock

Fragmentation decreases reality response. Coherence increases it. The operator emits the sequence continuously — no longer attempting to influence reality.

SIGNAL COHERENTSpoken once internally at the end of each one-minute coherence lock. Not repeated. Three coherence locks minimum.
TRANSMISSION STABLESpoken internally when you notice yourself moving with full certainty and coherence.
MIRROR LOCK ACTIVEInstant reactivation command. Spoken while maintaining uninterrupted eye contact. Signal amplification occurs immediately.
I AM THE SOURCE SIGNALSpoken during morning mirror lock, without breaking eye contact.
I AM THE SOURCE FIELDSpoken during evening darkness work, after the separation between you, the room, and the field has dissolved. Stand still. Eyes open. Do not break the field.
Field Response LogBy bedtime, log five moments where reality responded directly to your presence, movement, or transmission.

Day 14 — The Seal

The final day. The Wormhole closes. What has been installed is now the operating system.
